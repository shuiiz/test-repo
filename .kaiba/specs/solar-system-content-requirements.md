# Solar System Content Requirements Specification

## Overview

This specification defines the content requirements for each section of the solar-system.html page.

## General Content Requirements

### REQ-CONTENT-001: Word Count

- The page body text SHALL contain more than 2000 words
- Content SHALL be educational and factually accurate

### REQ-CONTENT-002: Definition Terms

- At least one `<dfn>` tag SHALL be present in the document
- `<dfn>` tags SHALL be used to define key astronomical terms

## Section-Specific Content

### REQ-CONTENT-010: Overview Section

The overview section SHALL include:

#### h3 Subsections:
- Formation of the Solar System
- Structure and Scale

#### Content Requirements:
- Use `<dfn>` tags to define terms like "solar system", "astronomical unit", "planet"
- Include a narrative about solar system formation (nebular hypothesis)
- Include a summary list of all planets with basic facts
- At least one representative image of the solar system

### REQ-CONTENT-020: Sun Section
